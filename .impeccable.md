# Vichellins Nation — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
General Lagos public — broad age range, mixed tech comfort levels, many buying gadgets online for the first time or occasionally. They're not power users; they need clarity and reassurance. They're shopping on mobile as often as desktop. They care about authenticity (is this product real?), delivery reliability, and price. The admin panel is used by the store owner and a small staff team managing orders, inventory, and the catalogue daily.

### Brand Personality
**Energetic · Friendly · Genuine**

Voice is warm and direct — like a knowledgeable friend who knows gadgets and gives you straight advice. Not corporate, not overly casual. Confident without being intimidating. Copy should feel like it was written by a real person in Lagos, not a generic e-commerce template.

Emotional goal for storefront visitors: **excitement and discovery** — "Wow, look at all these gadgets, I want to browse." But the *structure* should feel calm and spacious so that excitement comes from the products, not from clutter.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Product-led calm with energetic colour.** Inspired by Apple Store's spaciousness and product focus — generous whitespace, products as heroes — but warmed up with Vichellins' royal blue and bright gold identity.

The brand colours are non-negotiable and distinctive:
- **Marine blue** (`#1212b0`) — vivid royal blue, not dark navy. Primary brand colour.
- **Gold** (`#f5c800`) — bright, warm gold. Used for accents, CTAs, highlights.
- **Surface** (`#f4f5f7`) — light grey canvas.

Typography: Geist Sans. Clean, modern, friendly — works well. Don't swap it out without reason.

Anti-references:
- **Not** Jumia/Konga — no rainbow banners, no deal-spam, no busy grid overload
- **Not** generic Shopify dark-mode with glowing accents — that's not Lagos, it's nowhere
- **Not** corporate telecom blue — Vichellins' blue is vivid and proud, not conservative

Light mode only. The brand is bright and energetic — dark mode would undermine it.

### Design Principles

1. **Products are the heroes.** Every layout decision should serve product discovery. Generous space around product cards. SVG illustrations should feel deliberate and considered, not placeholder-ish.

2. **Calm structure, energetic colour.** Whitespace does the heavy lifting. The royal blue and gold provide the energy — the layout should be restrained so the colour pops.

3. **Built for Lagos, for everyone.** Design for varying tech literacy. Clear CTAs, simple navigation, no jargon. Touch targets must be generous. Mobile is first-class.

4. **Trustworthy by default.** Every page should answer the unspoken question: "Is this place real?" Authenticity badges, warranty callouts, and social proof should be visible without being pushy.

5. **Friendly, not formal.** Rounded corners, approachable typography weights, warm copy tone. The admin panel can be more utilitarian, but even there, the brand colour should anchor it and feel cohesive with the storefront.
